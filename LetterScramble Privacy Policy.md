# Privacy Policy for LetterScramble

**Effective Date:** May 4, 2026

LetterScramble is a simple text creation tool that allows users to arrange words based on the number of rows and the word direction they choose. Users may also save messages within the app for later use.

## Information We Collect

LetterScramble does **not** collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

The app does not require users to create an account, provide an email address, or submit any personal data.

## Saved Messages

LetterScramble allows users to save messages for later use. These saved messages are stored locally on the user’s device.

Saved messages are not uploaded to our servers, shared with third parties, or used for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

If the app is deleted, locally saved messages may also be deleted depending on the device’s storage behavior.

## Advertising and Tracking

LetterScramble does **not** display advertisements.

LetterScramble does **not** use tracking technologies, analytics tools, cookies, or third-party advertising services.

## Third-Party Services

LetterScramble does not use third-party services that collect user data.

## Children’s Privacy

LetterScramble does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Since the app does not collect data, no personal information is gathered from children.

## Data Security

Because LetterScramble does not collect or transmit user data, there is no personal data stored on external servers. Any messages saved by the user remain on the user’s device.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this policy.
